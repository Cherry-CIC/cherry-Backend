## Financial retention is category-specific

For a limited company, GOV.UK describes keeping company tax/accounting records for six years from the end of the financial year concerned, with circumstances requiring longer retention. This can support a rule for necessary accounting evidence once cherry's entity and obligations are verified. It does not require keeping every customer profile, photograph, phone number or shipping address. [GOV.UK accounting records](https://www.gov.uk/running-a-limited-company/company-and-accounting-records)

Companies House separately describes a three-year accounting-record baseline for private companies and six years for public companies. Tax obligations can require longer retention. A CIC is a type of limited company; being charity-first does not establish charitable status or a tax exemption. Record the actual applicable obligation in the approved schedule instead of selecting the shortest or longest number without justification. [Companies House guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/filing-your-companies-house-accounts/life-of-a-company-part-1-accounts), [GOV.UK social enterprise structures](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-a-social-enterprise)

## Retention matrix

The bases/exceptions below are candidates for review, not blanket authorisation. Article 6 bases and Article 17 exceptions are separate questions. A contractual or legitimate-interest purpose alone does not automatically defeat a valid erasure request. Restricted IDs and hashes remain personal data where linkage or identification is possible. [ICO storage limitation](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/storage-limitation/)

Protected job keeps the UID only as required to finish work and apply restrictions | Restrict on acceptance; erase promptly during worker processing; proposed live maximum 28 days from receipt | Revoke sessions, remove Authentication, recursively remove profiles/addresses. Job state must survive Authentication deletion |

When both order retention periods expire, live identifiers, amounts, shipment records and private evidence expire together. A minimal history shell may retain the active counterparty UID and order state for that account's service purpose; their later closure removes it. No departing-user pseudonym or provider link remains in that shell.

Backend coverage and external work are separated in [the deployment runbook](firebase-deployment.md). A row in this matrix is not a claim that every external system or app cache has been configured.

## Provider instructions and evidence

**Firebase/Google Cloud.** Delete the Authentication account, related Firestore data and owned Storage objects as separate operations. Read-only metadata identified Stripe extension `0.3.4` and its six functions; its versioned deletion behaviour is documented in [the cloud preflight](cloud-preflight.md). Other trigger behaviour and actual deployed rules still require verification. Firestore parent-document deletion does not itself establish subcollection erasure. Backups, exported datasets, logging and Storage versions need configuration and verification outside application code.

**Stripe.** Map Customers using verified account ownership, not email alone. The baseline reused a Customer by email, which can let a new account inherit a previous account's saved payment relationship. Inspect legacy mappings before provider erasure. Stripe's Customer deletion API also cancels active subscriptions, and retained history can still be retrieved; do not call it blindly or equate it with erasure of all Stripe data. [Stripe Customer deletion](https://docs.stripe.com/api/customers/delete)

The current project also runs signup synchronisation through the Stripe extension, creating a separate Firestore `customers/{uid}` payment-data path. Its `Do not delete` setting disables both automatic provider-deletion callbacks; Authentication deletion does not remove these Firestore copies. Extension and backend Customer mappings must both be inventoried. See [version-specific evidence and the unresolved collection inventory](cloud-preflight.md).

Stripe acts as processor for some processing and controller for other processing. Use the applicable agreement and product context to determine the instruction/notification route, keep a restricted acknowledgement reference, and direct users to Stripe's privacy route for its independent-controller records when appropriate. Do not promise a universal Stripe retention period. [Stripe DPA](https://stripe.com/legal/dpa), [Stripe privacy portal](https://privacy.stripe.com/privacy/home)

**Sendcloud and carriers.** The inspected backend sends recipient contact/address details and stores parcel/label/tracking references. Sendcloud's terms identify it as processor for customer shipping data. Its current retention table lists orders up to 180 days, shipments/support/audit up to 365 days, integration logs 30 days and tracking-email history 90 days. These provider defaults do not approve cherry's own retention or prove a specific erasure request has been fulfilled. Confirm its request procedure and the carrier's separate role; cancellation of a shipment is not an erasure API. [Sendcloud terms](https://www.sendcloud.com/terms-conditions/), [Sendcloud retention](https://support.sendcloud.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360025137891-Data-Retention-Policy)

**Resend and email recipients.** Current main sends transactional emails, including shipping labels as attachments. The recipient address is not the only personal data: a seller's message can contain a buyer's name/address in the label. The deletion workflow creates private account, legacy-alias and relevant order review tasks. Verify the provider's applicable processing terms, retention and erasure procedure, identify necessary ongoing order/dispute evidence, and record the acknowledgement or justified exception. No automatic provider erasure or delivered-mailbox erasure is implemented. Do not mark a case resolved merely because the local email address was removed.

**Sign in with Apple.** The frontend offers Apple login. Firebase Authentication deletion alone does not prove Apple authorisation was revoked. The account deletion journey must obtain/revoke a supported Apple authorisation token/code and record a safe success/failure outcome. Never retain or log the credential after use. [Apple account deletion](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/)

**Other recipients.** Inventory finance exports, charity reporting, delivery carriers, support tools and any marketing/analytics integrations before production. Where erasure notification duties apply, record the recipient, request timestamp and acknowledgement or justified exception. Do not email third parties automatically from this implementation without a configured, approved process.

## Store and response requirements

Apple requires discoverable in-app deletion initiation, deletion of unnecessary associated data, proportionate verification, transparency about delays and completion confirmation. Deactivation alone is insufficient. Ordinary app users should not have to contact support just to initiate deletion. [Apple guidance](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/)

Google Play requires qualifying apps to provide in-app and external web request routes and complete the Data safety deletion disclosures. The web route must work, prominently identify the deletion pathway and reference the app/developer shown in the listing. [Google Play guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en-EN)

Review these sources again at release. The ICO pages currently flag review following the Data (Use and Access) Act. None of the draft periods above is a store approval guarantee or a universal legal deadline.
